## The brief

The Foundation for Reformed Theology was established in memory of Dr. John H. Leith (1919-2002), Calvin scholar and Presbyterian minister, to carry forward his work of equipping clergy and serious students of Reformed theology. The organization runs annual ministerial study seminars and maintains a growing library of theological resources for ordained ministers, seminary students, and church elders.

Their existing site was not keeping pace with the depth and credibility of the work. The resource library had no clear path through it. Seminar information was scattered across pages without a coherent structure. Visually, the site did not read with the authority the organization carries in its field.

  did="Separate the work into three areas: the seminar program (what they run, who teaches, how to apply), the resource library (searchable by topic and format), and the organizational story (history, leadership, and Dr. Leith's legacy). Staff maintain all three from the WordPress admin."

For a content-heavy, staff-maintained site where editorial quality matters and developer time is limited long-term, WordPress was the right platform. Bricks Builder gave me design precision without locking the team into an interface that fights them during content updates. ACF handled structured data for the resource library, so the team can sort and filter materials by topic, date, and format.

The design borrows from academic publishing: burgundy drop caps to open major sections, fleurons as section dividers, and a duotone treatment for archival photography. The goal was a site the Foundation would be comfortable handing to a visiting scholar without apology.

The full build, including the pieces above:

- Seminar program pages with faculty listings, schedule details, and a multi-step application workflow
- Resource library organized by topic, format, and date with ACF-powered metadata for filtering
- Editorial design system: burgundy drop caps, fleurons, and duotone archival image treatment throughout
- Organizational history section drawing on Dr. Leith's correspondence, publications, and public record
- Staff and board directory
- Donation and giving page connected to the Foundation's existing giving processor
- Contact and inquiry form
- RankMath configuration for theological search terms, calibrated to the audience rather than maximum crawl coverage
