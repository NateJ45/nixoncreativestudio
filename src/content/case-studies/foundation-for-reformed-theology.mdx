## The brief

The Foundation for Reformed Theology was established in memory of Dr. John H. Leith (1919 to 2002), Calvin scholar and Presbyterian minister, to carry forward his work of equipping ministers and serious students of Reformed theology. It gathers small groups of pastors for week-long study seminars, year after year, and keeps a library of bibliographies, Calvin Studies papers, publications and Dr. Leith's own recorded lectures.

I first rebuilt the site in spring 2026, then redesigned it from the ground up in October 2026 around the thing visitors come for: the library. The goals were the same both times. A minister should find the right paper or lecture in seconds, a prospective member should understand the seminars at a glance, and a staff with no technical help should be able to keep all of it current.

The redesign renders every page from a theme I wrote for it, with the library's records kept as structured fields so each resource knows its collection, author, topics, year and format. That structure is what lets the search filter by topic and person, the collections show live counts, and each paper describe itself to search engines as a scholarly article or a recording.

The full build, including the pieces above:

**For readers**

- A library-first redesign of every page: navy and warm paper, Source Serif 4 and Hanken Grotesk, framed seminar photographs, and a home page that opens on the search
- Five collections (bibliographies, John Calvin Studies, Foundation publications, sermons and lectures, worship leadership), each with its own page, live counts and latest additions
- Full-text search with suggestions, collection scope, topic and person filters, and a Cite link on each result
- Authors and topics indexes from A to Z
- Resource pages with the whole text on the page, a running head that shows the section you are reading, three text sizes and a copy link for each section
- An audio archive of Dr. Leith's sermons and lectures with series and decade filters, a player on every recording and a full transcript underneath
- Seminar pages, testimonials from participants with their full letters, and a path to an inquiry on every seminar page
- The history of the Foundation and of Dr. Leith, and the officers and directors
- A mailing list link on every page and a small giving card, so the two asks are always one tap away
- Scholarly structured data on each resource and share images drawn for each one

**For the staff**

- A Home screen in the admin with task buttons, what needs a look, recent changes with an Undo link, and what readers searched for and did not find
- Add a resource by answering What are you adding?, with only the fields that kind needs, an upload box that fills in the details, topic tick-boxes and a Before you publish list
- A five-step guided form for seminars, with leader-name suggestions and the year and status filled in automatically
- Undo and redo on every screen, a daily email of what changed, warnings for a duplicate file or title, and no way to delete anything permanently by accident
- Contact form messages with a spam check, reply buttons and the pages a visitor read before writing
- A Site Guide with step-by-step recipes and captioned tutorial videos for each job
- The admin itself in the Foundation's navy and gold, with plain names and a menu grouped by task
