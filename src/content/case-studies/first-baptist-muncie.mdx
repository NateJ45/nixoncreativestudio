## The brief

First Baptist Church Muncie was founded in 1859 and meets at 309 East Adams Street, in a Late Gothic Revival building completed in 1929 and on the National Register of Historic Places since 1988. I had built the church's earlier website on Wix. This time the job was a full rebuild onto Astro, Sanity and Cloudflare Workers.

The new site had a few jobs, in order. Make a first visit easy. Help people find the church's people and ministries. Keep the church's history and every one of its blog posts. And let the staff edit the pages themselves, without a site builder in the way.

The content lives in Sanity, and the Studio is built into the site at /studio. An editor picks a page, sees it as visitors will, and changes the words or the sections right there, with a live preview of the draft before it goes out.

The full build, including the pieces above:

- A home page that opens on this Sunday, with a slideshow of up to twelve photographs of the church that holds still for anyone who prefers reduced motion
- A Plan a visit page that walks through the morning in order, from check-in to Sunday school, the donut hour and worship, with room numbers
- A Ministries page for children, youth and adults, plus the children's newsletter (The Kid's Corner) and the youth newsletter (The Moose's Message) as pages of their own
- The church's history from 1859 to today, and an architecture page for the building
- A blog of sermon previews, news and longer pieces from the pastors, filterable by category and by Bible passage
- A What's On page of events, classes and gatherings, and a weddings page
- The church's PDFs fetched from Sanity once and served from Cloudflare R2
- An in-site search that loads only when someone opens it
- Sanity Studio built in at /studio, where every page is made of sections the staff can add, reorder or remove
- Type checks, linting, link checks, Playwright with axe accessibility runs, and Lighthouse on every change
