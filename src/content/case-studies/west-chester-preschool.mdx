<Chapter title="The brief">

<Lede study={props.study} />

West Chester Preschool is a parent co-op preschool in West Chester, Ohio, founded in 1969. Families help in the classroom alongside two teachers, and the families also run the school, including everything the public sees. Volunteers change every year or two, so the public side of the school tends to get whatever time the current parents have left over.

When I took on publicity, the school was still called West Chester Cooperative Nursery School, its Instagram had about six posts spread over three years, there was nowhere to buy a school shirt, and the website had grown to 15 pages that were hard to edit. Parents choosing a preschool look at all of it, so I worked on all of it.

<Decision
  title="A name parents would search for"
  easy="Keep the long legal name and add a tagline."
  did="Lead with West Chester Preschool, the words a parent actually types into a search box, and keep co-op in the logo so the thing that makes the school different is still front and centre."
/>

<Decision
  title="A store that runs itself"
  easy="Order a box of shirts once a year and sell them at pick-up."
  did="A print-on-demand store on Fourthwall at store.westchesterpreschool.org. Families order any size or color any time of year, every product is made when it sells, and nobody has to store, count or hand out stock."
/>

<Decision
  title="Social built from what happens in class"
  easy="Post a flyer whenever there is a fundraiser."
  did="Film and photograph real class days and events, then cut them into monthly recap reels and named series. Every piece carries the same fonts, colors and end card, and every one is checked so no child without a photo release appears."
/>

<Decision
  title="A website volunteers can keep"
  easy="Rebuild it on my own stack, the way I build client sites."
  did="Stay on Squarespace, which the school already uses, and rebuild every page from native blocks so text, photos and buttons all edit in the normal page editor. The only code left is a short, commented block of CSS for finish."

Everything I made for the school:

- **Name and logo:** the move from West Chester Cooperative Nursery School to West Chester Preschool, and a reworked logo with white and navy versions for light and dark backgrounds
- **Online store:** 49 products across youth, women’s, men’s and accessories, each with the logo set up for its own colors and print area, on the school’s own store address
- **Social media:** monthly recap reels, event reels and fundraiser posts, built on a shared template so every piece looks like it came from the same school
- **Photography and video:** hundreds of photos and clips from class days, events and fundraisers, edited and handed over for the school to use in its advertising
- **Fundraising site:** a [flyer maker](https://wcpfundraising.pages.dev/popcorn/) for the popcorn sale, the flower sale and Dine to Donate at City BBQ, where each family makes its own print, feed or story flyer with a QR code that opens its own shop
- **Website:** cut from 15 pages to 7, with the class facts, the state license number and inspection link, a longer FAQ, search titles and descriptions on every page, and 10 old addresses redirected to the page that now answers them

### Behind the decisions

The constraint that shaped everything was the next volunteer. Every choice had to pass one question: could a parent with no design background keep this going next year? That is why the store makes its own stock, the social pieces come from a template, and the website is all native blocks. The work I do for paying clients is the same, I just usually do it one piece at a time.
