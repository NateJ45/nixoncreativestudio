## The brief

First Presbyterian Church of Orangeburg is a Presbyterian congregation in Orangeburg, South Carolina, whose tagline puts the mission plainly: "Above all to glorify Jesus Christ." The church needed a website that could serve two very different groups at once. Visitors showing up for the first time want to know when services are, what to expect, and how to get there. Active members want to find ministry information, read the week's bulletin, give online, and book the building for events. The site had to do both jobs from the same front door.

The church had active ministry programming across music, children, youth, adult education, and community outreach, but none of it was organized in a way that helped someone understand what the church does in a normal week. Staff also needed something they could maintain without developer help.

<Decision

The site follows three audience lanes. Visitors land on clear worship information and an expectations guide before anything else. Members get a ministry section that covers every program the church runs, plus a Building section for facility reservations and a Giving section tied to OnRealm. The Updates section, with the blog, event calendar, and weekly prayer focus materials, serves both groups depending on what they are looking for.

The full build, including the pieces above:

- Home page with worship times, directions, upcoming events and the latest updates, so a returning member sees what is new this week
- Worship information page covering Sunday service times, the online viewing option, and what first-time visitors can expect, with answers to the questions a first-time guest asks
- Ministry section with pages for music, children, youth, adult programs, service teams, and community outreach
- Building section with a facility reservation system for organizations wanting to use the church space, including an online request form
- About section covering beliefs, a pastor's note, and the staff directory
- Updates section with weekly worship bulletins, weekly prayer focus posts, and a full event calendar
- Giving page connected to OnRealm for online giving
- Contact page with office hours and location information
