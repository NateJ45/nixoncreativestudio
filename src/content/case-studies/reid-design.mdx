## The brief

Staci Perkins runs Reid Design out of Plainfield, Indiana, serving homeowners across the Greater Indianapolis suburbs with everything from a single consultation hour to full-service room transformations. The work is strong. The existing online presence was not.

The brief had two parts. First, make the portfolio work as a sales tool, not just a gallery of nice rooms. Second, build something Staci can keep updated herself as new projects finish. Her ideal outcome was a site that does real qualification work before a lead ever calls her.

Interior design clients almost always have two blockers early on: they do not know what things cost, and they do not know whether their taste matches what the designer actually does. I built around both. The Style Quiz does on the creative side what the calculator does for price: it identifies a visitor's design archetype and primes both the client and Staci for a more productive first call.

I chose Astro and Sanity so Staci can add new projects, publish journal posts, and update press coverage without touching a line of code.

## What I built

- Portfolio with before/after comparison sliders and Room x Style filter chips so visitors can find relevant work fast
- Budget Calculator with room type, scope, and add-on selectors that outputs an estimated project range
- Style Quiz that identifies a visitor's design archetype and primes them for the consultation call
- E-design service page for productized remote design, built with a toggleable coming-soon state while Staci prepares the offering
- Lead magnet download hub with email capture for guides and planning resources
- Affiliate shop ("Shop My Favorites") organized by room type with FTC disclosure built into the page design
- Journal with seven custom content block types covering project walkthroughs, product roundups, and process breakdowns
- Press coverage strip for earned media and magazine features
- Resources hub that ties the quiz, calculator, and guides together in one place so visitors can self-serve before reaching out
- Full Sanity CMS for portfolio projects, journal posts, press entries, and site-wide configuration
