Staci Perkins runs Reid Design out of Plainfield, Indiana, serving homeowners across the Greater Indianapolis suburbs with everything from a single consultation hour to full room design and styling.

The brief had 2 parts. First, show how Staci works and what it costs, so the people who reach out already know what to expect. Second, build something Staci can keep updated herself. Her ideal outcome was a site that does real qualification work before a lead ever calls her.

- Home page that leads with Staci, her Google rating and real reviews from Google and Facebook, then the 4-step process with honest time estimates
- Services page with notes for builders and realtors, and the service area
- E-Design page for clients outside the drive radius: the same process, delivered online
- Contact page with an optional room and style picker drawn as paint chips, and a link to book a free call first
- A visual system taken from an interior designer's working day: paint chips, sample tags, fabric swatches and a tape measure
- Editing screens for pages, services, photos and site-wide settings, so Staci can change a price, a page or a photo herself

### Behind the decisions

Interior design clients almost always have 2 blockers early on: they do not know what things cost, and they do not know whether their taste matches what the designer actually does. I built around both. The prices are on the page, and the contact form asks which rooms and which paint-chip styles feel like home, from Transitional to Japandi, so Staci starts the first conversation already knowing the brief.
