Built with

Custom-coded in Astro 7 with TypeScript, and deployed to Cloudflare. Every page is built ahead of time as plain HTML, and the case studies are text files that live with the code. No page builder, no stock template, no lock-in.

Typography

Everything is set in Hanken Grotesk, served from this site, not from a font service.

Color

A near-white page, near-black ink and one blue of my own, for headlines, buttons and the closing panel. Every other color on the site is a client's, inside the window, sheet or section that shows their work.

Accessibility

Built toward WCAG AA and checked automatically on every deploy. In plain terms: it works with a keyboard and with a screen reader. There is a fuller statement on the accessibility page.

Performance

Every page is static HTML, so it stays fast on a phone and a slow connection.

Screenshots

Every client site shown here is a real, live site. The screenshots and footage are captured from the live pages by a script that lives with the code, not mocked up.

Privacy

Two analytics tools: Cloudflare's, which sets no cookies, and Google Analytics, with its advertising features left off. No advertising trackers. The privacy page lists everything the site collects.
