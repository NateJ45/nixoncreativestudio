Your name, built like the city.

Four ways to make the name itself the logo, all drawn on the real Hanken Grotesk letters. These are first drawings, for judging the idea.

The Bridge

Your name starts and ends with an n, so those letters become the towers of the Roebling Suspension Bridge, and the cable spans the whole word. Only your name can do this.

My pick. It keeps the name fully readable, it is unmistakably Cincinnati, and a bridge is a good picture of connecting people to what they came for.

The Skyline

The stems of the letters keep climbing to different heights, with stepped Art Deco tops, and a beacon floats over the i. The word is the skyline.

The most literal answer to your question, and the most striking big. It gets dense when small.

The Backdrop

The plain name stands in front of a skyline in your blue. Nothing about the letters changes.

The easiest to read and the most flexible, but the least ownable, since any city could use it.
