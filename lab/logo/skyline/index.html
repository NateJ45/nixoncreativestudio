N, for Cincinnati.

Five ways your initial could come from the city. These are first drawings for judging the idea, not finished logos.

The Roebling

The two stone towers of the Roebling Suspension Bridge are the stems, and half a span of cable falls between them as the diagonal. A bridge is a good picture of the job, too: connecting people to what they came for.

My pick. The only one where the N was already standing there.

Two Towers

The stems are skyline: a stepped Art Deco tower like Carew on the left, and a crowned tower like Great American on the right.

The most literal skyline. Locals will spot it, but it gets busy.

48322416Nixon Creative Studio

Lit Windows

A downtown tower at night, with the lit windows spelling N. Windows are pixels, so it is the city and the web in one picture.

The cleverest idea. At 16 pixels it simplifies to a grid, which suits it.

48322416Nixon Creative Studio

The Hidden N

A horizontal skyline over the river. Look again, and the tallest buildings are an N.

Wide, so it works in a header or footer. Needs a separate small version.

The Terminal

The half dome of Union Terminal, with its fan of ribs and the N standing in the doorway.

The most loved building in town, and the most Art Deco of these.
