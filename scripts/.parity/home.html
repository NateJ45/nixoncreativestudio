How we work

Four steps, from the first conversation to launch day.

  1. 01

    Conversation

    We meet for an hour, in person or on video, and talk about what you actually need. No pitch deck, no agency theater. Just a working session that ends with a one-page brief we both agree on.

  2. 02

    Strategy

    I write the audience map, the page architecture, and the conversion path. You read it, push back, we revise. The strategy doc is the source of truth for every design choice that follows.

  3. 03

    Design and build

    Components, content, and pages built in the open. Preview links land in your inbox at every milestone so you can react early instead of seeing the whole thing for the first time at launch.

  4. 04

    Launch and follow-through

    We go live together, then I stay close for the first month to fix anything that surfaces. After that you own the site outright, with documentation and a simple way to reach me when something comes up.
