# Styling: kit.css, page CSS, tokens, motion

Loads when styles, components, layouts, scripts or pages are touched. The design rules and the reasons for them are in `DESIGN.md` (repo root); read it before visual work. This file is the where and how.

## The files

- `src/styles/kit.css` is the shared stylesheet, imported once by `BaseLayout.astro` (and by `coming-soon.astro`, which does not use the layout). It holds the `@font-face`, the tokens, the top line, the dock, tiles, the client sheet, the closing, quote bands, the price hand, `.label`, `.giant` and `.pip`. It is plain CSS in the order things were added, with later blocks refining earlier ones, so search for a selector before adding a second rule for it.
- `src/styles/elevate.css` is the second shared file, imported by `BaseLayout.astro` straight after `kit.css`: rolling digits, rising headings, the grain, the sheen, the progressive blur, the cursor label, the first-load drawing, the tier morph, `.vh` (visually hidden, global), thin scrollbars and the `.giant` top trim.
- `src/styles/elevate-b.css` is imported once by `SiteTools.astro` (so it ships on every page as its own small stylesheet): the link previews (`.peek`), the palette (`.pal`, `.pal-open`), the sound switch (`.snd`, `.fc-snd`) and the client sheet's grip. It adds no token and no hex beyond `#fff` on `--fill` (the `.chip.go` pair).
- `src/styles/pages/*.css` is one file per page or family: `home` (the home sections after the grid, imported by `index.astro`), `about`, `churches`, `sector` (the lean sector pages, and the Try it rules that /churches/ shares), `colophon`, `legal` (privacy and accessibility), `prices` (the /services page), `start` (the /contact page), `study` (every case study), `sub` (journal list rows), `photography` (the light table), `journal` (the journal index and posts), `work`, `lab` (the Lab on /work), `one-page`, `404`. Shared pieces live in `src/styles/` beside `kit.css`: `seg-bar.css` (the ink-fill chips, /work and /journal), `stamp.css` (the rubber stamp and the `.stamp.peel` that turns up a second line: /contact, About and journal posts), `ephemera.css` (the drawer of paper objects in `src/components/ephemera/`: crew list, film strip, ticket; each component imports it, and where an object sits stays in the page's own file), `receipt.css` (the till-slip card, imported by LiveReceipt and PostReceipt), `price-turn.css` (the price hand's turn, imported by `PriceHand.astro`, so Home and /churches/) and `tier-picker.css` (imported by `TierPicker.astro`, so every page). Each page imports its own file in its frontmatter. Do not put page rules in `kit.css`.
- There is no Tailwind, no utility classes, no `globals.css` and no CSS-in-component framework. A new component's style goes in the page file that uses it, or in `kit.css` if two or more pages share it.

## Tokens

Declared in `:root` at the top of `kit.css`:

`--ground` (page), `--ink` (text), `--soft` (supporting text), `--card` (white), `--line` (hairlines), `--accent` (studio blue `#2f3fe6`), `--accent-soft`, `--fill`, `--on-ink`, `--on-ink-soft`, `--chip`, `--chip-ink`, `--dock`, `--wash`, `--well`, `--zebra`, `--sunk`, `--screen`, `--rim`, `--display` and `--body` (both Hanken Grotesk), `--gut` (page side padding, `clamp(1.25rem, 4vw, 4rem)`), `--edge` (the side padding that puts a full-bleed block's contents on the 90rem page's left edge), `--type-h1`, `--type-h2` and `--type-h3` (the display scale; use them rather than a new clamp) and `--ease`.

Declared in `elevate.css`: `--sun-x` and `--sun-y` (unitless, on `<html>`, written by `light.js` from the hour in `America/New_York`: `--sun-x` runs -1 at 6 AM through 0 at noon and midnight to 1 at 6 PM, `--sun-y` is 1 at noon and 0.35 at night; 0 and 0.5 with no script), `--lean` (`calc(var(--sun-x) * -6px)`, a shadow's sideways offset), `--grain` and `--grain-ink` (the noise tiles; surfaces that keep light tokens on the dark side, `.end`, `.band`, `.sheet`, `.study`, take the dark-speck one there) and `--bp-dur` (850ms, the first-load drawing).

- One typeface: Hanken Grotesk, one variable file at `public/assets/fonts/hanken.woff2`, preloaded in `BaseLayout.astro`. `brand/fonts/` holds two static `.woff` weights for the OG generators only; opentype.js cannot read a variable woff2. Do not add a second face. While it loads, `Hanken Grotesk Fallback` (three `@font-face` bands at the top of `kit.css`: Arial, Arial Bold or their Linux twins, scaled per weight band) draws the text at Hanken's widths so nothing shifts when it arrives (2026-11-16, /now was 0.14 CLS on a slow load). If the font file or the headline weights change, re-measure: block `hanken.woff2` in Playwright and compare headline heights and the CLS spec.
- Two themes, light and dark, under `data-theme` on `<html>`; DESIGN.md section 2a has the rules. Dark tokens are set again in `:root[data-theme='dark']` inside `@media screen`, dark-only rules sit in the same kind of block in the file that owns the surface, and light must stay pixel-identical when dark changes. `--accent` is blue text, `--fill` is a blue surface. A client's color on the neutral page is `var(--cm, var(--c))`. A new card's `box-shadow` starts with `var(--rim)`. There is no navy and no amber.
- A client's colors never become tokens. They arrive as `--c` (accent) and `--p` (paper, and the text color on the accent), set inline on a `.tile`, a `.band`, the sheet or the study `<body>` (`bodyStyle` on `BaseLayout`).
- A new hex or token pair clears WCAG AA and is asserted in `src/lib/kit-tokens.test.ts`; a client pair in `src/lib/case-palettes.test.ts`.

## Patterns to reuse

- **Label voice**: `.label` (small, uppercase, tracked, tabular numbers).
- **Big line**: a `.giant` inside a `.big` container, sized with `--fit` (wide) and `--fitm` (phone) in container-widths; add `data-weigh` for the scroll-driven weight gain, and `data-build` (below-the-fold lines only, never a page-head H1) for the words rising in on scroll. Check 320px.
- **Deck**: `.pip` and `.pip.flip` in two opposite corners of a portrait card, one `.pip` alone on a wide box, `.keyed` for the inner rule, `.pc` for a price card, `.tile` for a client window. No suits.
- **Hairlines for non-cards**: `border-top: 1px solid var(--line)` on lists, tables and FAQs. Do not box them.
- **Color**: the page is neutral, the studio blue is `--accent`, a client's color stays in their own surface. A `.band` and the `.end` closing never touch: keep a neutral section between them.
- **Cards** are `.card` or their own radius (1.25rem; bands and the closing 1.75rem; buttons fully round).
- **H1 reveal**: words are wrapped in the markup by `wordSpans()` (`src/lib/words.ts`; `PageHead.astro` does it). `.js .w` animates from opacity .02. This is the one allowed start below full opacity in the first viewport.
- **The closing's parts**: `.end-work` (names and the white bar, `[data-pull]` per row, in `kit.js`), `.end-index` (the ruled site index, a new link goes in one of its groups), `.end-name` (one `<i>` a letter, clipped per word), `.end-base`. Text on the closing is white or sits on white; use `--fill` for blue on white.
- **Reveals for study blocks**: `.rv` is hidden only under `.rv-on` (added by `study.js` when motion is allowed and `IntersectionObserver` exists) and shown with `.in`. Nothing in the first viewport takes it.
- **A figure** (`roll.js`): `data-roll` on the innermost element whose only content is the figure's text (`<strong data-roll>$4,500</strong>`); the HTML holds the real final value and it rolls once, in place, only if its top was below the fold at load. For a figure script changes, `ui.roll(el, 'new text', opts)`: `el` must hold text only (its children are replaced); `opts.from` is the text to roll away from when the old figure lived in another element, `opts.delay` waits, `opts.still: true` sets the text with no roll and stops a running one (use it on the non-animated path), and `ui.roll(el)` rolls the current value in place. Mixed text works ("26 to 41", "$4,208 over 1 year"); numbers are paired in order and lined up from the right. `el.textContent` is the plain value at every moment (the moving cells are `aria-hidden` and draw their glyphs from `data-n` and `data-o`; the value sits in `.roll-sr`), so tests and screen readers never see it doubled. Every `[data-roll]` is set in tabular figures; an element only ever passed to `ui.roll` sets `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` itself.
- **Rising headings** (`rise.js`): every `<h2>` inside `<main>` rises on its own; `data-rise` on an `h3` gives it the same, `data-rise="off"` opts one out. It leaves alone a heading on the first screen, in a `dialog`, `[popover]`, `form`, `summary`, `button`, live region, `[hidden]` or `.rv` block, one holding `.w`, `.bw` or `[data-roll]`, and one not laid out at load. A heading that would wrap differently once split is left alone, and one your script rewrites while it waits is let go at once. `ui.words(el, cls)` in `kit.js` is the shared splitter (`data-build` uses it with `bw`, this with `rw`).
- **The cursor's caption** (`cursor.js`): `data-cursor="Word"` (one or two short words). `data-cursor=""` switches it off for an element and everything inside. Given automatically, so do not add these by hand: `a[target="_blank"]` (New tab), `[data-clip]` (Play), `[data-ba] .ba-f` and `[data-table] [data-stage]` (Drag), `[data-otc-turn]`, `[data-otc-back]`, `[data-lab-turn]` (Turn); `Tile.astro` writes Open on every window. A selector that is the same on every page goes in the `AUTO` list at the top of `cursor.js`. The course line's own position hook is `data-cl-cursor`, so it never collides with the caption.
- **Grain**: class `paper` on a surface that is not a `.card`. It sets only `background-image`, so a later `background` shorthand wipes it; set `background-color` instead.
- **Sheen**: `data-sheen` on an element with `position: relative` or `absolute` (round corners need `overflow: hidden` or `clip`), whose own `::after` is free. For markup added after load, call `ui.sheen(el)`.
- **The hour's light**: `var(--lean, 0px)` as a shadow's x offset; `var(--sun-y)` to deepen a drop at night. Never required.
- **The time in Cincinnati**: `<span data-cincy hidden>... <span data-cincy-time></span></span>`. `light.js` writes the time, removes `hidden` and keeps it right each minute.
- **A sound as something lands**: `ui.cue('deal' | 'tick' | 'turn' | 'print' | 'stamp' | 'toggle' | 'open' | 'close', delaySeconds?)`. Always safe to call: it is a no-op with sound off, and `kit.js` carries a stand-in until `sound.js` installs the real one.
- **Link previews**: none to build. `peek.js` gives every link to a study, `/services/` or a post its preview by href. A link that already shows its own preview, or sits beside the thing it links to, takes `data-peek="off"` (on it or an ancestor).
- **Late-loaded helpers**: in the built page the order is component scripts, then the layout's script (`kit.js` and the shared modules), then the page's own script, so a page script can call `ui.roll` and `ui.sheen` directly. A component script can run before them; `kit.js` carries plain stand-ins for that case (`ui.roll` sets the text, `ui.sheen` does nothing).

## Class names already taken

Search before naming a class. Collisions that have bitten: `.dealt` is the dealt price layout in `kit.css` (a study's landed decision uses `.landed` instead); `.fan`, `.rail` and `.more` already mean something on the sheet and the case studies (the dock uses `dk-` and `fc` prefixes); `.jump .jump-row` on /services is written with the parent on purpose, because `seg-bar.css` loads after `prices.css` and its `.dw-seg { flex-wrap: wrap }` would otherwise stack the chapter bar five rows deep on a phone; `dialog.pal.card` is written with that specificity because `.keyed` sets `position: relative` later in `kit.css`. `body::before` and `body::after` belong to the first-load drawing (`html.bp`), and a `[data-sheen]` element's `::after` to the sheen.

## Motion

- Springs are written in JavaScript: `ui.spring(k, c, v)` in `src/scripts/kit.js`, and the per-page scripts use it. CSS motion uses `--ease` or a springy bezier; keep durations short.
- Page changes are cross-document view transitions (`@view-transition { navigation: auto }` in `kit.css`; the dock keeps `view-transition-name: dock`). There is no router.
- `study-hero` and `tier-card` are the two borrowed names, both lent and given back. `tier-card` goes from a price card (`a.pc-face`) to its tier on /services (`price-morph.js`): the card takes it in `pageswap`, an inline `<head>` script in `BaseLayout.astro` reads the sessionStorage note `ncs-tier-morph`, holds the first paint until the tier is parsed (`<link rel="expect" blocking="render">`), names `#<tier> .t-card` in `pagereveal` and takes the name off when the transition ends; `html.tier-morph` tells `prices.js` to lay the tiers out at once. `study-hero`: `src/scripts/hero-morph.js` (imported by `Tile.astro` and `study.js`) puts it on the window you picked in `pageswap`, the study's `.s-fan .p-home` wears it in `study.css`, and a sessionStorage note read by an inline script in `[slug].astro` tells the study to show the pane instead of throwing it. Two elements with one name cancel a transition, so anything that takes it must give it back. The /work grid and list morph uses temporary `dw-<slug>` names under `html.dw-morph` (`work.js`, `work.css`).
- Every moving thing has a reduced-motion answer: read `ui.reduce` in script, and add the selector to the `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` blocks in CSS. Under reduce the view transition and smooth scroll are off, springs start at rest, footage stays a still, and nothing runs after load (`tests/reduced-motion.spec.ts`).
- The dock's motion (puck, rail, hands) is its own block in `kit.js`. `ui.spring`'s `step` takes an optional third argument, a stiffness for that step, which the puck uses so the leading edge is the quicker one. Class names in the dock are prefixed `dk-` or `fc`: `.fan`, `.rail` and `.more` already mean something on the sheet and the case studies.
- A hover that lifts its card uses `ui.stillHover` in `kit.js`, so the card cannot lift off the pointer and jitter.
- Frame loops that follow the scroll keep to three rules (the 60 fps pass, November 2026), or the page drops frames on an ordinary laptop:
  - Write inline styles through `ui.put(el, prop, value)`, which skips a value that has not changed. A loop wakes on every scroll event and most frames work out the same numbers.
  - Ask where something is with `ui.top(el)` and `ui.height(el)` (and `ui.pageH()` for `scrollHeight`), not `getBoundingClientRect()` or `offset*` in the middle of a frame. Read after another loop has written, each of those makes the browser lay the page out again. `ui.top` remembers the page position and re-measures when the page changes size or the scrolling stops; anything inside a sticky or fixed box is always measured live. If a loop must measure, measure everything first, then write.
  - Put a per-frame custom property on the element that uses it, not on a big container: a custom property is inherited, so setting one on a section restyles everything inside it. Register it with `@property { inherits: false }` when only that element reads it (`--bs` on `.band-bg`, `--bk` on `.band-w`).
  - Call `ui.top(el)` once when a loop is set up, before any scrolling: the first measure of an element otherwise lands mid-frame and lays the page out.
  - Anything that changes text size (a font weight, a font size) lays the page out each time it changes. `[data-weigh]` moves in steps of 20, not 1.
  - A scroll-driven CSS animation should animate `transform`, `scale`, `rotate`, `translate` or `opacity` on the element itself, which the browser runs off the main thread, not a custom property that children read (the case-study opening, `s-settle-*` in `study.css`).
  - Looping decoration that animates SVG (About's map) holds still while the page scrolls (`.cm-hold`, client-map.js) and carries on when it stops.
  - Anything that moves with the scroll itself should be a scroll-driven CSS animation (`animation-timeline: scroll()`), with script only measuring, as the home deal and the case-study opening are. A frame loop always lands a frame behind a phone's own scrolling.
  - When an element's own place on the screen drives it, use a named `view()` timeline on the thing that does not move (the band, the big line, the closing's stage and name) and let the moving parts read it (`animation-timeline: --band`), with `animation-range: cover <length>` stretches in `vh` that match the old script's fractions of `innerHeight`. Put a timeline on an ancestor, or widen it with `timeline-scope`; a moving element's own `view()` shifts as it moves. A spring the pointer drives stays in script as one registered, non-inherited number the keyframes read (`--gp`, `--gh` on the closing). `kit.js` decides with `sda` (scroll-driven animation and motion allowed) and keeps the frame loop for everything else; `kit.css` asks the same in `@supports` and the reduced-motion query. A smoothstep is `linear(0, 0.028 10%, 0.104 20%, 0.216 30%, 0.352 40%, 0.5 50%, 0.648 60%, 0.784 70%, 0.896 80%, 0.972 90%, 1)`. Shrink a rounded surface with `clip-path: inset(m round r)`, not `inset` or `scale`, so nothing is laid out and the corners keep their radius (the quote band).
  - Hover waits while the page scrolls (`ui.stillHover` in kit.js): cards sliding under a still mouse used to lift and start their clips mid-scroll. The card under the pointer is taken on the first real move after the scroll stops.
- There is no Pause motion button and no `data-motion` attribute (the client sheet's looping clip and About's map each have their own pause button). Footage starts only on hover or, on a touch screen, while a window is centred, and stops when it leaves. The map is the one thing that loops by itself, only while on screen, and only under its `[data-live]` attribute.

## Forced colors and print

A card is drawn with a shadow and a background, so `kit.css` gives `.tile`, `.card`, `.pc-face`, `.chip` and `.dock` a 1px `CanvasText` border under `@media (forced-colors: active)`. A new surface whose edge is only a shadow joins that list (`tests/forced-colors.spec.ts`). The two top-layer surfaces from `elevate-b.css`, `.peek` and `dialog.pal.card`, start their shadow with `var(--rim)` and carry their own forced-colors border in that file. Forced colors keep background images, so `elevate.css` switches the grain, the blur, the sheen and the first-load drawing off by hand; a new decorative background image joins that block. Print hides the top line, dock, sheet and closing, and drops the grain, the blur and the rise wrappers.

## Top line

`TopLine.astro` is the name, the phone theme switch (`.turn-top`, shown at 40rem and under, its own pill when floating) and the status button, plus the `.reach` popover. `kit.js` adds `.float` and a transform (the pills on scrolling up) and splits the name into `.lt` letters on first use. Colors are tokens, so it follows the theme; `.top.float.on-color` puts it back on `--ink` once it has left the client's color. A new bar that sticks to the top of the screen joins the `topBar` selector in `kit.js` (today `'.dw-bar, .toc, .jump'`), so the pills do not land on it.

## Fixed elements

The viewport meta carries `viewport-fit=cover`. The dock (`Dock.astro`, design in `DESIGN.md`) sits at `bottom: max(0.9rem, env(safe-area-inset-bottom))` and the mailto toast above it. A new `position: fixed` element at a screen edge adds the matching `env(safe-area-inset-*)`.

The `.pblur` layers (`Dock.astro`, `TopLine.astro`) sit at `z-index` 38 (bottom) and 29 (top) with `pointer-events: none`; a new fixed element at the bottom or top edge goes above them, or it is blurred. Keep them siblings of the nav and the header: never put a `backdrop-filter` inside the dock (it holds `view-transition-name: dock`, and a backdrop filter inside a named element blurs nothing), and never give an ancestor of a `.pblur` an `opacity`, `filter` or `mask` (which is why the top one slides in instead of fading). The tier picker's corner card (`TierPicker.astro`) is rendered after `<Dock />` and outside `<main>`. The client sheet takes an inline `transform` and the class `dragging` while a finger drags it.
