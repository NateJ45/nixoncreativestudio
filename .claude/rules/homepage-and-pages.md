# Homepage and page architecture

Moved out of CLAUDE.md. Loads when page, component, layout or lib files are touched. Covers the homepage section order and every CMS-DESIGN PR that moved a page onto the CMS.

## Homepage architecture

Rebuilt in the 2026 redesign from the approved d7a direction (`docs/redesign-2026/mockups/d7a-carousel-contact-sheet`). Components live in `src/components/home/`; `src/pages/index.astro` only reads the case studies once and lays the bands out. Section order is the IA, and each band has its own ground and its own composition (DESIGN.md "Grounds"):

1. **Hero** (`HomeHero.astro`, `paper-contours`, the page's one moving ground, under the header). Nathan's tagline in Bebas with the italic voice turn ("I make websites that / _pull their weight._"), then one paragraph: who it is for and the volunteer promise (`hero_positioning`) followed by the proof sentence with its "one person" link to `/about/` (`hero_proof_*`), then "Start a project" (`/contact/`) and "Prices published, from $4,000" (`hero_secondary_label` plus the lowest tier from `getPricingTiers()`, linking to `#prices`). The proof sentence is one template expression so no formatter can put a space before its comma; the price label is built as one string for the same reason. Server-rendered, no reveal, nothing at opacity 0.
2. **Hero reel** (`HeroReel.astro`, inside the hero). The fanned carousel of real client sites: a horizontal scroll-snap row whose frames take their place in the fan from a CSS view timeline (pure CSS; Chrome, Edge, Safari 26), a plain snap row where unsupported, never auto-advancing. Order: Stone Steps (the start frame), then the FBCM build as the church proof ("Launching soon", unlinked), Foundation for Reformed Theology, Theology Matters, MAS Monograms; second pages to the left. The arrows ride a film strip under the fan, and from 1100px they sit at the centre frame's sides so they show in a 900px-tall first screen. About 1.4 KB of script adds arrows, Left/Right/Home/End on the focused reel, mouse drag, click-to-centre, the counter, and the promotion of parked frames. The start frame (Stone Steps home page) is the LCP element: a real `<img>` in a `<picture>` (phone capture under 700px, desktop above, AVIF then WebP through the Images binding), eager, `fetchpriority="high"`, sized. Every other frame is parked (srcsets in `data-` attributes, a blank placeholder, a `<noscript>` real copy) until the load event and an idle beat, or the moment the visitor touches the reel: with them loading beside it the LCP measured 2.66 s, parked 1.98 s (Gotcha 14). Captures: `src/assets/home/reel/`, re-shot by `node scripts/brand/capture-home-reel.mjs` (FBCM from its workers.dev preview, never from fbcmuncie.org).
3. **The proof sheet** (`ProofSheet.astro`, `window-light`, still). One film strip per live site (ink rebate, sprocket rows, facts printed on the edge, the keeper circled in china marker) with a short note and one proven fact. Stone Steps leads, larger, the strip left and the note right; Foundation for Reformed Theology runs full width with the note below; Theology Matters puts the note on the left; MAS Monograms has a three-frame strip. Crops: `src/assets/home/sheet/`, loaded through `DeferredPicture.astro` (parked until scrolled to, or 3.5 s after load). Heading and intro: `work_heading` / `work_sub`, split into caps and voice by `splitVoice()`.
4. **What it costs** (`HomePrices.astro`, `deep`, still, `#prices`). The three tiers from `getPricingTiers()` as a hairline-ruled price list (name and who it is for, the floor in Bebas, what grows at that tier; the "Recommended" tier shows its badge and a red figure), then what every build includes, the reassurance line and the link to `/services/`. The typical ranges stay on `/services` only. Static numbers, no count-up.
5. **How it works** (`HomeProcess.astro`, `paper`). The four `process_steps` as a timing sheet on one ruled line, each step's duration in red Bebas, and a china-marker bracket over steps two and three ("6 to 10 weeks, kickoff to launch"). The duration labels are in code (`DURATIONS`): the schema has no field for them; they restate the step copy and the `/services` FAQ, so change them together. `/services` draws the same steps with `services/ProjectTimeline.astro`.
6. **Who you work with** (`HomeAbout.astro`, `deep`, still). The bundled headshot, the kept line "The person you meet on day one is the same person who hands you the finished site." in serif italic, a two-paragraph bio, two real snapshots and "More about me". These words are in code (the home entry has no field for them); they restate the About page's story, so change both together.
7. **Closing call** (`HomeClose.astro`, `paper-contours`, still). `process_cta_title` ("Let's build a site you won't have to redo."), `process_cta_sub`, the button and the email address (`getSite()`; the mailto keeps its fallback).
8. **Footer**, rendered by `BaseLayout` on the `deep` ground.

**Which work shows** is curated in `src/lib/homeWork.ts` (`REEL`, `SHEET`, unit-tested) and gated by the case studies, read once in `index.astro`: a site whose study is not live drops out of both (a `soon` reel slide needs its study to be "Launching soon", is labelled "Launching soon" and is never linked); the proof sheet also needs "Featured"; "Read the case study" appears only when the study exists, so a dataset without the row (local dev, CI) never links a 404. Second Pres, Reid, the Academy and Orangeburg are not in the curation at all. Every fact in a caption or note is one D's copy audit marked proven (`docs/redesign-2026/D-copy-positioning.md`); do not add a number that is not on that list. The case studies' "Show in the homepage device scene" and order fields (`in_hero`, `hero_order`) no longer drive anything on the homepage.

**Headlines.** `splitVoice()` turns a CMS heading into the Bebas lead and the italic voice: after the first sentence break, else after the last comma, else the last four words of a line of seven or more. Keep that in mind when editing a heading in the admin, and mind the schema's length limits (`cms/schema/page_home.mjs`, checked by `cmsContent.test.ts`).

Services, photography and testimonials live elsewhere: `/services` and `/about` carry the offering, `/photography` the photographs, and quotes belong inside case studies. Retired with the rebuild: the WebGL hero, the device-pairing `HeroShowcase`, `ClientMarquee`, `PricingTeaser`, `src/lib/heroSites.ts`, and (once `/services` stopped reusing them) `ProcessBand` and `SelectedWork`.

Since the 2026 redesign `/services` draws the same four CMS steps with their durations on a week scale (`src/components/services/ProjectTimeline.astro`), so the words still read identically across the site; the homepage sets them as a timing sheet (`HomeProcess.astro`). The `CtaBanner` component is the tail-of-page inquiry block on `/about` and `/photography` (`/services` and the homepage have their own closing bands).

Since CMS-DESIGN PR 8 the words, photos, Currently lists and Lighthouse numbers on `/about` are the **About page** CMS entry (`getAboutPage()` in `src/lib/aboutPage.ts`, committed fallback `cms/content/page_about.json`, schema `cms/schema/page_about.mjs`). **Contact redesign (2026-10-04, branch page-contact): the shortest honest path to an inquiry.** `/contact/` is three bands: `window-light` (the page's one moving ground, under the header: the plain headline, "first conversation free / I read every message myself / reply within one or two business days", and the email and phone links), `paper` (the form beside the "What happens next" ledger, which is the CMS `next_steps`), and `paper-contours` (still: "Working from <site.address>", the CMS `where_text` and closing CTA). The Ohio contour ground IS the location: the Google Maps iframe is gone, so the page makes no third-party request (a test asserts it). No street address is published. No booking link exists in site settings, so the page only shows "pick a time" when `site.bookingUrl` is set; never invent one. Turnstile was never on this page.

- **Required: name, email and the message (min 20 characters). Everything else is optional** and sits in a native `<details data-contact-more>` ("Tell me more"): organization, type, current site, budget, timeline, phone, how-you-heard. The script opens the details when a restored draft or the `?org_type=` preselect fills one. Budget used to be required; it no longer blocks a send. Field names, ids, the label-as-value rule, org-type codes, the honeypot `botcheck`, hidden fields, the draft restore and the `astro:page-load` re-bind guard are unchanged.
- **Validation** is the script's: `form.noValidate` is set in JS (not in the markup, so no-JS visitors keep native validation), each required field has a `<p data-error-for>` wired with `aria-describedby` and `aria-invalid`, focus goes to the first bad field. Inputs are 48px, 16px type (no iOS zoom), with `inputmode`/`autocomplete`/`autocapitalize` set.
- **Conversion hook.** On a successful send (not a honeypot hit) the script: replaces the URL hash with `#sent`, ticks the first ledger step, and dispatches `window` CustomEvent `ncs:contact-sent` with `detail { orgType, budget, timeline, heardFrom }` (labels only, never name, email or message). No analytics vendor is wired, so a tag listens for it, e.g. GA4: `window.addEventListener('ncs:contact-sent', (e) => gtag('event', 'generate_lead', { budget: e.detail.budget }))`; Plausible: `plausible('Inquiry', { props: e.detail })`. (A GA4 tag that is wired but dead is Nathan's open item.) A `/contact/thanks/` virtual URL was rejected: a refresh of it would 404.
- Copy that is in code, not the CMS: the three reassurance lines, form labels, hints, error text, the thank-you panel. Changing the CMS `heading` or `intro` needs a CI dataset refresh (`scripts/ci-dataset/cms-fixtures.mjs`) because `tests/contact-copy.spec.ts` reads them from `cms/content/page_contact.json`.

Since CMS-DESIGN PR 11 the words on `/work`, `/photography`, `/journal` and the 404 page are four more singleton entries (**Work page**, **Photography page**, **Journal page**, **Not-found page**; readers in `src/lib/indexPages.ts`, committed fallback `cms/content/page_work.json`, `page_photography.json`, `page_journal.json`, `page_not_found.json`, schemas `cms/schema/page_*.mjs`), and the photography page's pictures are the new **`photos`** collection (`getPhotos()` in `src/lib/photos.ts`, schema `cms/schema/photos.mjs`, admin group "Photography"). **`photos` has no committed fallback on purpose**: zero photos, a read error and a missing table all give the page's designed zero-photo state (below), which is what production shows until Nathan adds the first photo in the admin. Pictures are served as resized WebP through width-only `/_image?href=...&w=N&f=webp` URLs (never a height, or a tall photo meets the 4096 px resizer limit in `docs/EMDASH.md`): the hero and cards use `EmDashPhoto`, the justified gallery gets `srcSet` and a larger `lightboxSrc` through `PhotoGallery.tsx`. The Journal entries are the `posts` collection since PR 12 (next paragraph). Still in code: the Work page layout (see "/work and the case studies" below), the project count, the Photography band headings, booking steps and "Where these photographs were taken" note, the Journal note's buttons.

Since CMS-DESIGN PR 12 the **Journal** is the template `posts` collection, relabelled (admin: Journal; schema `cms/schema/posts.mjs`: `title`, `featured_image` as Cover image, `content` as Body, `excerpt` as the required 200-character Summary, `updated`; tags are the built-in `tag` taxonomy). `/journal/` and `/journal/<slug>/` read the PUBLISHED entries through `getJournalEntries()` / `getJournalEntry()` (`src/lib/journal.ts`); `/rss.xml` carries them beside the case studies; `src/pages/sitemap-posts.xml.ts` is the journal sitemap (valid and EMPTY while nothing is published, because the stock EmDash handler answers 404 and the index would list a broken child); `generate-og.mjs` builds a navy `og/journal/<slug>.png` per entry from `/rss.xml`. **There is no committed fallback, on purpose**: zero entries, a read error and a missing table all read as an empty journal, so `/journal/` shows its "first entry is coming" card and the Journal menu item stays hidden (`hasJournalEntries()`, memoised per request, tags every page with `posts` so the first publish purges the nav everywhere). **A draft is never visible**: an unknown slug and a draft slug both answer a real 404; the CI dataset carries one draft (`ci-draft-entry`) so tests catch a leak. The publish date is EmDash's `published_at`, shown in `America/New_York`. **The body is drawn by `renderJournalBody()` (`src/lib/journalBody.ts`), not by `emdash/ui`'s `PortableText`**: importing that component made its 9.5 KB stylesheet the shared `ui.*.css` chunk and loaded it on the homepage, `/services`, `/work` and every case study (found by parity, 8/14 PASS; the PR 8 trap again). Supported: paragraphs, h2/h3 with ids, quotes, nested lists, bold, italic, inline code, safe links and code blocks (plain monospace boxes, focusable, NO syntax colouring). **Pictures, tables and embeds in a body are not drawn yet**; the cover image is the picture an entry has. A new entry's share card exists only after the next deploy (build-time script). Still in code: the Journal note's buttons and the "Filed under" label.
