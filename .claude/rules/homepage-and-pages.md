# Homepage and page structure

Moved out of CLAUDE.md. Loads when pages or components are touched.

## Homepage architecture

The homepage renders in this order: Hero, Selected Work, What it costs (the pricing teaser), Process Band, then the Footer (from `BaseLayout`). Section order IS the IA, not decoration. The previous eight-section frame (Hero, SelectedWork, Services, PhotoStrip, Process, Testimonials, CtaBanner, Footer) was retired during the rewrite documented in PRODUCT.md; an impeccable critique flagged numbered-eyebrow scaffolds, three-stacked-card-grid monotony, and photo-shy presentation as the loudest AI tells on the page, and the rewrite undoes them.

1. **Hero** (`src/components/Hero.astro` + `src/components/HeroReel.astro`), "The Reel" (2026-10-30). The surface follows the theme (`bg-bg-soft` in light, navy `bg-primary` in dark), which is what the header's `data-over-hero` state paints, so bar and hero read as one block. Left: the headline "I build websites that pull their weight." set in Bricolage Grotesque at `--text-display` (the site's display face since package 1.5), one positioning line (sectors, "wherever you are", and "one person in Cincinnati" linking to /about), the brand "Start a project" pill and "See the work", then a "Now showing" row (one site's standout feature at a time, cycling every 3.8s, desktop only) with a Pause button. Right: `HeroReel`, a tilted 3D wall of five columns of real client homepages (desktop and phone captures mixed), drifting up and down at different speeds; hovering a column (mouse only) stops it and lifts the card to show that site's feature line. The one orchestrated moment is the headline: "pull their weight." animates from weight 200 to 800 on load, through `Heft.astro` (the site's weight signature since package 1.7, with the hero's own 1.8s timing). Each of those words sits in a one-cell grid with an invisible heavy copy that reserves its full-weight width, so the animation never causes layout shift, and only weight animates (never opacity), so the headline stays a clean LCP element. Below `lg` the copy comes first and the wall drops under it as a flatter tilted band (four columns). Motion rules: reduced motion means no drift, no ticker, heavy headline from the start, and the Pause button is hidden; otherwise the Pause button (WCAG 2.2.2) is a `data-motion-toggle` that sets the site-wide Pause motion state (`html[data-motion-paused]`, shared with the footer switch and remembered on the device), and the reel and ticker, both `data-motion="loop"`, also hold still off-screen and in hidden tabs. The wall is decorative (`aria-hidden`); its cards are mouse-only links to each case study (`tabindex="-1"`). Content: `src/data/reel.ts` (sites, feature lines, column layout). Images: `src/assets/reel/{slug}-desktop.jpg` (600x1240) and `{slug}-phone.jpg` (300x840), re-shot from the live sites with `node scripts/capture-reel.mjs`; Astro serves them as small WebP at 1x and 2x. Feature lines must be true and live (the case study's outcome line is a safe source). This replaced the WebGL flow (`HeroCanvas`), the two-device `HeroShowcase` and the `ClientMarquee` strip, which cut about 1.2 MB of three.js from the homepage.
2. **Selected Work** (`src/components/SelectedWork.astro`). Three featured case studies, asymmetric: one large primary card (4:3 image + body in a two-column grid at desktop, stacked on mobile) followed by two medium cards in a 50/50 row. It is collection-driven: the component reads `getCollection('case-studies')`, filters to `featured === true`, sorts newest-first, and takes the top three (newest is the primary). Cards render real optimized covers through `CaseStudyCover`, and each surfaces the case study's `outcome` line in `--link` color above the summary so the proof-point reads at first scan instead of buried in body copy. Curate the homepage by setting `featured: true` on exactly the three entries you want here. The whole list is an `<ol>` because curation order matters; each card is a single `<a>` so the click target covers image + body together.
3. **What it costs** (`src/components/PricingTeaser.astro`). A value-anchored pricing band between Selected Work and the Process Band. It leads with the value floor every build includes (a custom design you own, 100/100 accessibility, strategy and a content system, one person start to finish), then the three web tiers from `src/data/pricing.ts` as a hairline-ruled progression (NOT a card slab) with the Signature tier anchored ("where most projects land" plus a brighter accent price), each carrying its typical range and scope points. Prices count up on scroll (the real number is the static fallback), and a reassurance line (free first conversation, monthly payments) precedes the link to `/services`. Sits on the plain page surface so it does not become a second full band before the Process Band.
4. **Process Band** (`src/components/ProcessBand.astro`). A band that combines the four-step process and the inquiry CTA. It uses the theme-aware `.band-themed` surface: flat `bg-bg-soft` in light mode, flat navy in dark mode. Step numbers (01–04) are the ONLY deliberately numbered sequence on the homepage; they earn their place because the process is genuinely ordered. The CTA title is specific to audience ("Tell me what you are building.") and the button leads to `/contact`. Closes the page on one strong block instead of three stacked sections.
5. **Footer**, rendered by `BaseLayout`, is a `.band-ink` block in both themes (Nathan's decision 4, package 2.1), so every page ends on ink. It opens with the closer (the page's `closer` prop, see below), then the Work, Studio, Contact and Currently columns and the utility row. Full description in `components.md`.

Services, photography, and testimonials each live elsewhere, not on the homepage:

- **Services** copy lives on `/services` and `/about`.
- **Photography** has its own `/photography` page; on the homepage the work itself is carried above the fold by the hero reel, not by a four-up photo tile strip.
- **Testimonials** belong inside individual case study pages where the quote has earned context (and the /about carousel reads the same `testimonial` frontmatter). A standalone "what people say" band on the homepage at three equal cards put quotes at the lowest-impact layout for the format.

`ProcessBand` is one component used in two placements, so the four steps read identically across the site: the homepage closes with it (`variant="band"`, CTA on), and `/services` reuses it mid-page (`variant="soft"`, `showCta={false}`). The old standalone `Process.astro` was deleted in favor of this. Every page ends on the footer's ink closer: one static H2 (300 lead, `<b>` at 800, never Heft), an optional line, the email and the CTA pill, set per page through `BaseLayout`'s `closer` prop (`src/lib/closer.ts`: `heading`, `emphasis`, `sub`, `href`, `label`, `cta`). A page that passes nothing gets the default ("Let's build a site **you won't have to redo.**"). Today: /about, /photography, /services and /work have their own; each case study has a sector one ("Building for a **church?**") whose pill links to `/contact/?from={slug}&org_type={sector}`; /contact has a quiet one with no pill (`cta: false`), since a pill there would link to the page itself. The old `CtaBanner` component was deleted in package 2.1.

Don't reorder these sections. The hero no longer depends on any single content gate: the reel of real client sites fills it today, so it ships complete.
