# Homepage and page structure

Moved out of CLAUDE.md. Loads when pages or components are touched.

## Homepage architecture

The homepage's job is proof of craft, then fit, then price. It renders in this order (overhaul package 2.2): Hero, Selected work, Who I build for (`SectorIndex`), the testimonial slot (empty until the testimonials package fills it), What it costs (`PricingTeaser`), How I work (`ProcessBand`), then the Footer's ink closer (from `BaseLayout`). Every section below the hero is a `Section.astro` band with a mono rail, and the surfaces alternate white, paper, white, paper so each hairline marks a real change before the page ends on ink. Section order IS the IA, not decoration. The previous eight-section frame (Hero, SelectedWork, Services, PhotoStrip, Process, Testimonials, CtaBanner, Footer) was retired during the rewrite documented in PRODUCT.md; an impeccable critique flagged numbered-eyebrow scaffolds, three-stacked-card-grid monotony, and photo-shy presentation as the loudest AI tells on the page, and the rewrite undoes them.

1. **Hero** (`src/components/Hero.astro` + `src/components/HeroReel.astro`), "The Reel" (2026-10-30). The surface follows the theme (`bg-bg-soft` in light, navy `bg-primary` in dark), which is what the header's `data-over-hero` state paints, so bar and hero read as one block. Left: the headline "I build websites that pull their weight." set in Bricolage Grotesque at `--text-display` (the site's display face since package 1.5), one positioning line (sectors, "wherever you are", and "one person in Cincinnati" linking to /about), the brand "Start a project" pill and "See the work", then a "Now showing" row (one site's standout feature at a time, cycling every 3.8s, desktop only) with a Pause button. Right: `HeroReel`, a tilted 3D wall of five columns of real client homepages (desktop and phone captures mixed), drifting up and down at different speeds; hovering a column (mouse only) stops it and lifts the card to show that site's feature line. The one orchestrated moment is the headline: "pull their weight." animates from weight 200 to 800 on load, through `Heft.astro` (the site's weight signature since package 1.7, with the hero's own 1.8s timing). Each of those words sits in a one-cell grid with an invisible heavy copy that reserves its full-weight width, so the animation never causes layout shift, and only weight animates (never opacity), so the headline stays a clean LCP element. Below `lg` the copy comes first and the wall drops under it as a flatter tilted band (four columns). Motion rules: reduced motion means no drift, no ticker, heavy headline from the start, and the Pause button is hidden; otherwise the Pause button (WCAG 2.2.2) is a `data-motion-toggle` that sets the site-wide Pause motion state (`html[data-motion-paused]`, shared with the footer switch and remembered on the device), and the reel and ticker, both `data-motion="loop"`, also hold still off-screen and in hidden tabs. The wall is decorative (`aria-hidden`); its cards are mouse-only links to each case study (`tabindex="-1"`). Content: `src/data/reel.ts` (sites, feature lines, column layout). Images: `src/assets/reel/{slug}-desktop.jpg` (600x1240) and `{slug}-phone.jpg` (300x840), re-shot from the live sites with `node scripts/capture-reel.mjs`; Astro serves them as small WebP at 1x and 2x. Feature lines must be true and live (the case study's outcome line is a safe source). This replaced the WebGL flow (`HeroCanvas`), the two-device `HeroShowcase` and the `ClientMarquee` strip, which cut about 1.2 MB of three.js from the homepage.
2. **Selected work** (`src/components/SelectedWork.astro`, white). The three studies with the lowest `order` (the same order /work and the Up next loop use), each a `FramePair` of its real desktop and phone captures, alternating sides from md (7/12 frame, 5/12 copy). Beside each: a static H2 (a 300 lead from the sector, "A nonprofit site for", then the client name in `<b>` at 800; the name links to the study), the study's `hook`, mono meta (sector and year) and the live host with ↗ (new tab). The "peek": hovering a study, or tabbing to its links, pans the desktop frame down the real homepage capture (`FramePair peek`, CSS transform only, 6s glide, eased back on leave; nothing moves under reduced motion or `html[data-motion-paused]`). The frame is also a mouse-only link (`tabindex="-1"`, `aria-hidden`) carrying `transition:name="cover-{id}"`. The section's rail counts the studies, and it ends with "All {n} sites →" to /work/, n counted from the collection. A study missing its `-home.png` / `-mobile.png` capture or `liveUrl` is skipped. To change which studies show, change `order` in the frontmatter.
3. **Who I build for** (`src/components/SectorIndex.astro`, paper). One hairline row per sector that has a study: the plural name (`SECTOR_PLURAL` in `src/lib/closer.ts`) in Bricolage 300 and the count in mono, both counted from the collection. Each row links `/work/?sector={key}` with the collection's own sector keys (`church`, `school`, `nonprofit`, `small-business`), the same ones the /work filter reads. Hover and focus underline the name and slide the arrow.
   - **Testimonial slot.** A commented placeholder in `src/pages/index.astro` between Who I build for and What it costs, rendering nothing. One real quote, verbatim, goes there (testimonials package); swap the tones below it so the surfaces keep alternating.
4. **What it costs** (`src/components/PricingTeaser.astro`, white). Ruled, not carded: the value floor line ("Every build includes: ..."), then the three web tiers from `src/data/pricing.ts` as columns on a 1px `--line-strong` hairline. Signature (the `highlighted` tier) gets a 2px accent top rule, its badge "Where most projects land" in mono and its price at 800. Prices are static text (no count-ups, no raised card). Then the reassurance line and "See full pricing and services →". It owns a `variant` prop: `teaser` here; `full` on /services (each tier's paragraph and scope points, a "Start with {tier}" link to `/contact/?tier={name}`, the add-ons in a `<details>` whose summary carries their prices, and no link to /services).
5. **How I work** (`src/components/ProcessBand.astro`, paper). A weighted timeline with no numerals: the four steps hang off one vertical `--line-strong` rule whose 2px fill draws down as the band scrolls (scroll-driven CSS only, drawn in full under reduced motion, Pause motion or no support). Step titles are static 300 with the key word at 800 ("Start with a **conversation**"); no Heft (ruling 9). It has no CTA of its own: the home's `closer` prop carries its old line ("Tell me what you are **building.**").
6. **Footer**, rendered by `BaseLayout`, is a `.band-ink` block in both themes (Nathan's decision 4, package 2.1), so every page ends on ink. It opens with the closer (the page's `closer` prop, see below), then the Work, Studio, Contact and Currently columns and the utility row. Full description in `components.md`.

Services, photography, and testimonials each live elsewhere, not on the homepage:

- **Services** copy lives on `/services` and `/about`.
- **Photography** has its own `/photography` page; on the homepage the work itself is carried above the fold by the hero reel, not by a four-up photo tile strip.
- **Testimonials** belong inside individual case study pages where the quote has earned context. The home gets exactly one real quote, in the slot above; never a "what people say" band of three equal cards.

`ProcessBand` is one component used in two placements, so the four steps read identically across the site: the home (`tone="paper"`) and `/services` mid-page (`tone="white"`, `headingId="process-heading"`). The old standalone `Process.astro` was deleted in favor of this. Every page ends on the footer's ink closer: one static H2 (300 lead, `<b>` at 800, never Heft), an optional line, the email and the CTA pill, set per page through `BaseLayout`'s `closer` prop (`src/lib/closer.ts`: `heading`, `emphasis`, `sub`, `href`, `label`, `cta`). A page that passes nothing gets the default ("Let's build a site **you won't have to redo.**"). Today: the home ("Tell me what you are **building.**"), /about, /photography and /services have their own, /work has "Don't see your kind of **organization?**" with its pill on `/contact/?from=work`; each case study has a sector one ("Building for a **church?**") whose pill links to `/contact/?from={slug}&org_type={sector}`; /contact has a quiet one with no pill (`cta: false`), since a pill there would link to the page itself. The old `CtaBanner` component was deleted in package 2.1.

Don't reorder these sections. The hero no longer depends on any single content gate: the reel of real client sites fills it today, so it ships complete.

## /about (overhaul package 2.6)

The page's job is trust in the person. Order, in `src/pages/about.astro`:

1. **Head**: the real headshot (`src/assets/brand/headshot.jpg`, through `Media.astro` with `lcp`, 4:5) and the H1 with its Heft. The headshot is the page's LCP at every width, so it is never revealed: on a phone it sits above the H1 (visual order only; the H1 stays first in the DOM), and from lg the text takes columns 1 to 6 and the photo 7 to 12. This band is its own `.page-grid` rather than a `Section`, because Section's body starts at column 4, which leaves the photo too small to out-size the H1. The H1 uses `--text-h1-long` so "organizations" at 800 fits 320px. The H1 wording is Nathan's to pick (plan section 7); keep it until he does.
2. **In short** (`Section`, white): the thesis line and four plain facts. The rail's "{n} client sites" and the case-study fact are counted from the collection.
3. **The longer story** (`Section`, prose). The family "we" sits behind `data-copy-ignore`.
4. **Outside the studio** (`Section`, paper): a contact sheet of the five real photos in `src/assets/about` (the `photos` array: `src`, `caption`, `alt`), numbered 01 to 05 in mono, each a `[data-zoom]` button for the one `Lightbox`.
5. **Currently** (`Section id="now"`, white): the old /now snapshot. The rail shows the freshness ("Updated 4 weeks ago"), the body the "Last updated" date with a `data-motion="loop"` dot.
6. **How I work**: one line linking `/services/#why` (the "why it costs what it costs" block). The old "How I work" principles list was a repeat of /services and is gone.

Then the footer's ink closer ("Want to talk about **a project?**"). Deleted in 2.6: `Terminal.astro` and `LighthouseScore.astro` (the colophon carries the real numbers). Scoped CSS only.

## /work (package 2.3)

`src/pages/work/index.astro`. Its job is "find one like mine, then open it". The H1 is "{n} sites, **all live today.**" with n computed from the collection; if any study loses its `liveUrl` the heavy phrase becomes "**all shipped.**" by itself. The heavy phrase is the page's one Heft. Under it, `WorkFilter` (the `?sector=` tabs and the aria-live count, see `components.md`), then one ruled `<ol>` in `order`: each row is the client name in Bricolage at `clamp(2rem, 5vw, 4.5rem)` (Stone Steps, order 1, gets a larger lead row), the `hook`, sector and year in mono (`facts` overrides them), and two sibling links: the case study (the whole row, a stretched `::after` on the name link) and the live host with an arrow, which sits above the stretch. Never nest them.

On a fine pointer from md up, a pinned `BrowserFrame` sits on the right (sticky). Hovering or focusing a row tints it in the client's `palette.paper` with a 4px rule in `palette.accent` (light mode; `case-palettes.test.ts` checks this site's heading, body, muted and link colours on every client paper), or keeps it on ink with the client's dark mark as the rule (dark mode, ruling 8). The frame swaps to that site's full-page capture (`shots/{slug}-home.png`, cropped from the top to 2.4x its width with `getImage`, WebP at 480/720/1040) and pans down it once with the Web Animations API, 6s at most, pausing when the pointer or focus leaves. No pan under reduced motion or Pause motion. Captures are fetched only on first hover; the frame starts on the lead study's capture, lazy, so a phone never downloads it. On touch or below md the frame is hidden and each row carries a 16:10 `Media` thumbnail of the cover (lazy, except the first, which is `lcp`).

Images on /work measured 159 KB at 1440 and 89 KB at 390 after a full scroll (2026-10-31; the budget is 500 KB). The page ends on the closer; there is no "Open any of them" band and no card grid any more.

## /services architecture

Its job is to size the project and start it (overhaul package 2.5). `src/pages/services.astro`, in this order, every band a `Section.astro` with a rail, surfaces alternating:

1. **Hero** (white, no hairline): H1 "What I do, and **what it costs.**" with `Heft` on the heavy phrase, and one line under it.
2. **On this page**: a sticky sub-nav (Web design, Photography, Pricing, Care, FAQ) under the header, linking to each section's H2 id. `scroll-padding-top` on `<html>` (only while the sub-nav is on the page) keeps an anchored heading and any focused element below both bars (WCAG 2.4.11). A small script measures the header into `--svc-header-h`; without JS a fluid fallback runs a pixel or so above the header's real height. On a phone the links scroll sideways inside the bar, whose right edge fades out (a mask) to hint that more follow. Section rails stick below the sub-nav on this page. `tests/services.spec.ts` tabs to each link at 1440, 768 and 320, with and without JS.
3. **Web design** (paper, `#web-design`): one real capture in a `BrowserFrame` (the newest study the home does not show), the buyer-language points, then strategy as "what's included", set as type (no tile, no image; its JSON-LD Service keeps `#strategy`).
4. **Recently shipped** (white): the next two studies the home does not show (the home takes the three lowest `order`; the rest are sorted newest `year` first, then `order`), as small `FramePair`s with the hook and live host. All computed from the collection.
5. **Photography** (paper, `#photography`): copy, points, the price and note from `pricing.ts`, and "Ask about photography" to `/contact/?service=photography` (the /photography page is gated). No image: the photos in `src/assets/about` are of Nathan, not by him.
6. **Pricing** (white, `#pricing`): `PricingTeaser variant="full"`. Package 3.3's "Size up your project" goes straight after it.
7. **Care** (paper, `#care`): the four care levels from `pricing.ts`, ruled the same way as the tiers.
8. **How I work** (white): `ProcessBand`.
9. **Why one person** (paper, `#why`): four ruled points and the note on tools.
10. **FAQ** (white, `#faq`): native `<details>`; the FAQPage JSON-LD is built from the same array, so it equals the visible text (checked in `tests/services.spec.ts`).
11. **Ink closer**: "Not sure which tier **fits?**", pill to `/contact/?from=services`.

Every price on the page, in the FAQ and in the JSON-LD comes from `src/data/pricing.ts`; `tests/metadata.spec.ts` checks each JSON-LD price is visible (the add-on prices sit in the `<details>` summary, so they stay visible while it is closed).

## /contact (overhaul package 2.7)

Its job is a qualified inquiry in under two minutes. The H1 keeps its Heft; the reply promise is the one the page already made ("one or two business days"), and any new timing waits on Nathan.

- **Form** (plain HTML posting to Web3Forms, works without JS, in the shared `.frame`). Required: name, email, organization type, "What are you hoping to build?". Optional in view: budget chips (real radios) whose labels read from the tier floors in `src/data/pricing.ts`. Optional in a closed `<details>` "A few optional details": organization name, phone, current website, timeline, how you heard. The selects keep the Gotcha 11 outline ring.
- **Prefill** (script, `astro:page-load`): `?org_type=` picks the select, `?tier=launch|signature|flagship` picks the chip that starts at that tier's floor, `?service=` and `?from=` are carried. Four hidden fields go to Web3Forms: `from`, `tier`, `service` and `origin_org_type` (the org type the link named; the visible select is `org_type`). Only slug-shaped values are used, and a prefill never overrides a restored draft. Link here as `/contact/?from={page or slug}&org_type={sector}`, `/contact/?tier={tier}` or `/contact/?service=photography`.
- **Sidebar order**: What happens next, then "What I'll read" (a brief card that assembles from the fields; a plain summary for screen readers, `aria-live="off"`, hidden without JS since only the script fills it), then Direct (CopyEmail and the phone from `site.ts`), then Where I am (`ContactMap`, see `components.md`). "Rather book a call?" renders only when `site.bookingUrl` is set.
- **Success**: with JS, a fetch submit swaps the form for the success panel, focus moves to its heading, and it links two studies from the visitor's sector (padded with the next studies by `order` when a sector has fewer). Without JS, Web3Forms redirects to `/contact/?sent=1#sent` and CSS `:target` shows the same panel with the first two studies.
- No CtaBanner; the page ends on the footer's quiet closer with no pill. /privacy names the form fields, the hidden origin fields and the local draft, and says the map is no longer a Google embed.

## Utility pages (package 2.9)

- **/colophon, /privacy, /accessibility** use `DocumentLayout` (`components.md`): mono tag, an H1 with one Heft ("How this site is **made.**", "What this site **collects.**", "Built to be used **by everyone.**"), the intro, "Last updated", the sticky "On this page" list at lg and one prose measure. The colophon's facts are tied to the code: the Astro and Tailwind major versions come from `package.json`, the script and image limits from `tests/page-weight.config.json`, and "Last updated" is the build date. Its live measurement ledger, weight slider and grid inspector are package 3.4; `/colophon/parts/` stays the component check page. Its closer is "Want a site built **the same way?**"; /privacy and /accessibility take the default.
- **404** (`src/pages/404.astro`): its job is recovery. The H1 "This page **lost its weight.**" runs Heft in reverse (800 to 200, its end state under reduced motion, without JS and under Pause motion). Below it an empty `BrowserFrame` whose address bar shows the requested path (written with `textContent`), and a "Did you mean" list: the build passes the nav pages, the colophon, privacy and accessibility pages and every case study to a small script, which scores them against the last path segment with `src/lib/did-you-mean.ts` (Damerau-Levenshtein on the slug, its runs of pieces and its initials) and shows up to three. Without JS, or with nothing close, the list is every case study, from the collection. No floating numeral, no shine.
- **/coming-soon**: a still of the reel wall, the wordmark, one line and the email. Nothing moves (`ComingSoon.astro`).
- **/journal**: gated as before (the nav link appears with the first published entry). The index is a dated hairline list in a 40rem measure (date and reading time in mono, the title as a link, the summary), no cards and no covers; with nothing published it is one plain line with links to the work and the contact page. Journal entries set their prose in the same 40rem measure.
- **Footer edge in dark mode**: `--paper` and `--ink` are the same navy in dark, so the footer gets a 1px `--line` top hairline in dark mode only (scoped in `Footer.astro`).
