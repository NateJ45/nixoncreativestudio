# Pages: what each route is made of

Loads when pages, page scripts or page styles are touched. The route table with sources is in `content.md`. Every page uses `BaseLayout.astro`, imports its own stylesheet from `src/styles/pages/`, and ends on `<Closing lead="...">` (except /contact, the 404 and /coming-soon). Page scripts import `ui` from `src/scripts/kit.js`, are plain processed Astro scripts, and run on load. Every page reads and links correctly with JavaScript off; the script only moves things.

## How a page is built

- The H1 comes from `PageHead.astro` or is written by hand with `wordSpans()` (`src/lib/words.ts`) and an `aria-label` carrying the plain sentence, so each word can reveal. Heavy words are `<b>`.
- A client appears as a `Tile` plus `Caption`, from `getClients()`. A quote appears as a `QuoteBand`, read from a study's frontmatter. Prices come from `src/data/pricing.ts`.
- Identity, contact and the closing's two sentences come from `src/data/site.ts` (`firstConversation`, `replyPromise`).
- Keep a neutral section between a `QuoteBand` and `Closing`.

## The pages

- **`/` (`index.astro`, `home.js`).** The hero: the H1 "I build websites that pull their weight." (with the small `.me` portrait), the sub line with the starting price, and the hand of client windows, which scroll deals into the grid. Then the work section (a `.giant` line that spells the real study count, the ledger ("Since 2020 · One person · Cincinnati", then "Websites for:" with all four sector pages linked), "Tap any phone to look inside, or" and a "Show me a random one" button that opens a random client's sheet, the grid), one `QuoteBand` (Theology Matters), `DealtPrices` and the `Closing`.
- **`/work/` (`work/index.astro`, `work.js`).** Large desktop windows (`Tile` with `desk`), two across from 52rem, each with name, sector, the study's one-line `outcome`, Read the case study and Visit the site. A window's sheet has its address at `?client=<slug>`, here and on Home (`kit.js`). A sector filter (`?sector=church` filters on load and rewrites the URL as you click; an unknown sector falls back to all) and a Grid or List switch (`?view=list`). Picking a sector shows a link to that sector's page. Footage is fetched only when a window plays. Below: "What the site has to do", the studies indexed by job (`src/data/jobs.ts`); a study goes under a job only if its case study supports it. Without JavaScript the ruled list is the page.
- **`/work/{slug}/` (`work/[slug].astro`, `study.js`).** The title on the client's color with their laptop and phone, then the story beside a rail phone that scrolls through the real homepage as you read, then Up next (by `order`, wrapping) and a sector closing. The story is the MDX body. Shape and frontmatter: `content.md`.
- **`/churches/` (`churches.astro`, `churches.js`).** The first sector page. As you read four points, the church's real phone page scrolls in its window. Then "Try it": four questions as rows with chevrons under a "Tap a question" hint; a tap scrolls the Muncie phone page to the answer and says in words where it was found ("Found it about 1 scroll down, under What to expect"). Under 62rem the phone comes before the questions. It works under reduced motion too (the page jumps). Then the church studies, "Which tier usually fits" (the church row of `SITUATIONS`, the same list the lean pages carry, styled from `sector.css`), the price hand and the closing. Orangeburg's stops stay above its events band, which reads "No events at the moment" between events. Copy and metadata: `src/data/sector-pages.ts` (`CHURCHES`). A new sector page needs its own `.astro` file, an entry in `sector-pages.ts`, a line in `tests/routes.ts` and one in `scripts/generate-og.mjs`.
- **`/schools/`, `/nonprofits/`, `/small-business/` (`SectorLean.astro`, `sector.css`, `sector.js`).** The lean sector pages: one line of `.astro` each around a shared component, copy in `sector-pages.ts` (`SCHOOLS`, `NONPROFITS`, `SMALL_BUSINESS`), situations in `SITUATIONS`. The job in the buyer's language, the proof that exists (with how thin it is, said plainly), which tier usually fits, and the call to action. No tools of their own, and no client tied to a tier.
- **`/one-page/` (`one-page.astro`, `one-page.css`, `one-page.js`).** The summary to forward to a board or committee: what I do, the tiers with the five-year sums, what is in every build, If you ever move on, how to reach me. Prints on one US Letter page; anything added means something else comes out.
- **`/services/` (`services.astro`, `prices.js`).** The Prices page (nav label Prices). The first screen says what you get, then shows the three tiers as a ruled row (`.glance`), then the giant starting price. Each tier card leads with who it fits (`fits`) and what I take responsibility for (`responsible`), then the price, the same If you ever move on block (`yoursToKeep`, only what is true today) and the features last. Then what moves a price within a tier, the "If this sounds like you" table (`SITUATIONS`), the optional three-question picker, the first-year, three-year and five-year sums (`src/lib/tier-costs.ts`, computed, year one hosting included, so care starts in year two), "Why not a cheaper platform?" (concedes first, no claim that custom saves money, no named competitors), recent sites, add-ons, care plans (a table from 48rem up, a stack of cards below it, both from `carePlans`), how it works, the "In every build" and "Why one person" folds, and the FAQ. Offers in the JSON-LD come from `pricing.ts`; the FAQPage JSON-LD must equal the visible FAQ. A "Start with {tier}" link carries `?tier=`. On a phone (`prices.js`, up to 52rem) the sections marked `data-mfold` fold to a ruled list of their headings once the script runs: the tiers, add-ons and care plans stay open, and with no script or on a wide screen nothing folds. A link or an address pointing into a fold opens it. "Timelines", "In every build" and "Why one person" are FAQ-sized rows at every width.
- **`/contact/` (`contact.astro`, `contact.js`).** One question at a time with the brief phone beside it (up to 60rem, where it would stack under the form, the brief is hidden). A real Web3Forms post: with JavaScript the script validates in plain words, saves a draft, prefills from the URL and posts with `fetch`; without it every field shows and the form posts natively, then returns to `/contact/?sent=1#sent`, where CSS `:target` shows the success panel. Prefill params: `?org_type`, `?tier`, `?service`, `?from`, each checked against a slug pattern. No `Closing` here. It sends one `generate_lead` event and never a field value. `PUBLIC_WEB3FORMS_KEY` comes from the environment.
- **`/about/` (`about.astro`, `about.js`).** The longer story beside a stage of client windows and a portrait card, a giant line ("One person."), two quote bands (First Baptist Muncie, Stone Steps 50K) and "What I do, and what I don't", two cards lettered `+` and the minus sign.
- **`/photography/` (`photography.astro`).** Gated: noindex, out of the sitemap, the closing's site index and `/llms.txt` until the `photos` collection holds `MIN_PHOTOS` photos (`src/data/photography-gate.ts`). While closed it shows one portrait and "ask for samples". The gallery needs a new non-React build (`docs/PENDING.md`).
- **`/journal/` and `/journal/{slug}/`.** Empty, noindex and out of the sitemap until the first published entry (`src/data/journal-gate.ts`); the index and entries use `legal.css` and `sub.css`.
- **`/privacy/`, `/accessibility/`, `/colophon/`.** Plain text pages on `legal.css`. Their claims are tied to the code and hosting; `tests/colophon.spec.ts` checks the colophon names the real font and host. Update the text in the same change as the thing it describes.
- **`/coming-soon/`.** A standalone page (no `BaseLayout`), always live, used by the gate.
- **`/404`.** The not-found page: the 404 line, two links (work, home), and the first client window, knocked askew, which stands up when you reach for it (and the missing path shown as text). There is no "did you mean" list any more. `wrangler.jsonc` serves `404.html` with a 404 status.
- **Redirects.** `/now` goes to `/about/` (an Astro redirect); `/prices` goes to `/services/`, `/start` to `/contact/`, and the old site's addresses to their nearest page (301s in `public/_redirects`).

## Rules for editing a page

- Copy is first person singular and states only what is true and sourced. No capacity or scarcity claims unless Nathan confirms them.
- A new page: file in `src/pages/`, its own CSS in `src/styles/pages/`, an entry in `tests/routes.ts`, a title and description (`content.md`, metadata), and an OG card (`npm run og:pages` picks it up).
- Do not add a router, a theme, a second nav or a React island (never-break rules 7 and 8).
